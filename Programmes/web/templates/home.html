Welcome to DbCAS

DbCAS is a community‑driven, unified gateway to the rapidly expanding landscape of alternative‑splicing and transcript‑level resources. It brings together a curated collection of databases spanning isoform biology, long‑read sequencing, single‑cell transcriptomics, and broader transcriptomic diversity, presenting them within a single accessible, searchable platform. By consolidating these otherwise scattered resources, DbCAS helps researchers quickly identify suitable tools and datasets, navigate the complexity of isoform‑resolved data, and stay informed as new splicing‑focused databases continue to emerge.
